# Classroom Team Quiz

Build a teacher-controlled MCQ game for **IGCSE and AS Level only**. It will reuse the existing image-backed Cambridge question bank and PhysicsHQ styling, without changing the database.

## Game setup
- Choose **IGCSE** or **AS Level** from the grade page; A2 will not show or open the game.
- Choose **Random Mix** or **Topical** questions. Topical mode supports one or more available topics.
- Set 2–6 team names, question count, and time per question.
- Only questions with an image and answer key are eligible.

## Classroom round
- One projected teacher screen shows the question and countdown.
- The teacher records one A–D choice for each team; each choice locks for that round.
- The answer reveals automatically when every team locks or time expires.
- Correct teams receive base points plus a small speed bonus based on when their answer was locked.
- The teacher advances to the next question after reviewing the answer and updated leaderboard.

## Finish
- Show ranked team results, scores, correct-answer counts, and the winner.
- Offer **Play again** with the same setup or return to the grade page.
- Game progress remains on this device for the active session; separate team phones and online rooms are not included.
